## Verification standard applied

An item is kept only if a PRIMARY source (wire, mainstream outlet, official
site) dates it to 21 Nov 2026. Two cases both count, and the file says which
applies to each item:

- **(A) Event and primary publication both dated 21 Nov** (Saturday events
  reported Saturday).
- **(B) Friday event in the US/Europe whose primary wire publication is
  dated 21 Nov UTC** (the story reached the 21 Nov news cycle; e.g. an AP wire
  timestamped 03:46 UTC on 21 Nov, or a Washington Friday-noon event that
  fell on 21 Nov in Beijing). Aggregator-only 21 Nov timestamps do NOT count:
  several draft items were dropped for exactly that reason (see
  "Dropped/replaced").

Saturday 21 November 2026 is the local date in the UK/US and in WIB/UTC.
Friday 20 November 2026 in Washington/Europe is 21 November in Beijing/WIB
only for events late in the US day; morning events in Europe on 20 Nov are
still 20 Nov everywhere and are flagged honestly below where relevant.
